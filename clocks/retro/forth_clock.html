The program

        

        
Forth, from 1970, has no expressions and almost no syntax: words separated by spaces, each one acting on a stack of numbers. A colon defines a new word. The table on the left steps through the body of HMS>S, turning hours, minutes and seconds into seconds since midnight. The interpreter is WAForth, a Forth written in WebAssembly and running here in your browser.
