The program


        
Prolog programs are facts and rules, not steps. The --> rules are a grammar for the time in English, and Prolog can run a grammar in either direction: from a time to its words, or from words back to the time they name. Each second the page asks the queries on the left. The interpreter is Tau Prolog, written in JavaScript and running here in your browser.
