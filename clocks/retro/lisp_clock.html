The program


        
Lisp, from 1958, writes programs and data the same way: as lists in parentheses. Here the clock face is data, a list describing an SVG drawing, built by ordinary functions and then turned into markup by ->svg, itself a dozen lines of Lisp. The backquote ` writes a list as a template, with , and ,@ marking the parts to fill in. The interpreter is BiwaScheme, a Scheme written in JavaScript and running here in your browser.
