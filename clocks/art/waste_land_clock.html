I. The Burial of the Dead 

The day, by the poem's own clock

0006121824

The poem names its own hours: a winter dawn, the stroke of nine, the hot water at ten, a winter noon, a closed car at four, the violet hour, and closing time. The rest of the day is filled from the night of the fifth section.
