Cien años de soledad

Gabriel García Márquez, 1967 · seven generations of Buendías in one day
The hour belongs to
The first generation

    Melquíades's parchments 0%

    The day is the century. Each ring is a generation, eldest outermost, and the family steps inward one ring every 3 h 25 m until the line ends at midnight. Red marks every bearer of the name now in focus; the gold thread winding inward is Úrsula, who outlives five generations.
