The Quest of the Ring ran 185 days, from Frodo leaving Bag End to the Ring's unmaking, and here it runs once a day, about 7 minutes 47 seconds of clock time to each day of the road. In the Shire calendar every month has thirty days and the two Yule daysstand outside the months. Shadedspans are the long halts in Rivendell and Lothlórien.
