How near it comes through the day

In Vance's Dying Earth a deodand is a man-shaped thing with black skin and a taste for human flesh. It haunts the forests and talks smoothly to the travellers it means to eat. The rest is this clock's own reckoning: the red sun rises at six and sets at six, and the creature comes nearest at three in the morning.
