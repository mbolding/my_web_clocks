Literary 41

Works of literature reinterpreted as timekeeping devices.

Modernist 8

Bloomsday Clock Follow the 18 episodes of Joyce's Ulysses through June 16, 1904, with stream of consciousness and Dublin locations. Finnegans Wake A circular dream-logic clock shrouded in the murky waters of the River Liffey, where the time endlessly ripples and melts. The Waste Land Clock T.S. Eliot's five sections with fragmentary voices, drifting text, and modernist typography. Mrs Dalloway Clock Virginia Woolf's single June day in London 1923, with Big Ben chimes and stream of consciousness. À la recherche du temps perdu Proust's seven volumes with involuntary memory, madeleines, and time regained. The Sound and the Fury A four-quadrant clock displaying the exact same time through the vastly different, conflicting psychological states of four narrators. Snow Country A pristine, quiet aesthetic where the time reflects against a dark train window as endless snow gently drifts through a long night. The Magic Mountain Thomas Mann's sanatorium where time distorts. A mercury thermometer tracks each hour, while Hans Castorp passes through seven years above the flatlands.

Existential & Absurd 7

The Metamorphosis Gregor Samsa's transformation through stages of the day. Kafka's sparse nightmare of isolation. The Trial A confusing bureaucratic nightmare where reading the time requires deciphering infinite official forms and denied status appeals. The Stranger Meursault's indifference, the Algerian sun, the murder on the beach. "It was because of the sun." The Sisyphus Clock Camus' eternal boulder, pushed uphill every minute in a flat cutout-collage style — and squashed by a giant foot on the hour. Crime and Punishment Raskolnikov's feverish days, the murder, guilt, confession. Guilt meter tracks psychological torment. Catch-22 Clock Yossarian's missions and recursive time loops. The required missions always increment. Candide A relentlessly optimistic clock that ignores the violent CSS disasters happening behind it, while you quietly cultivate a procedural canvas garden.

Shakespeare & the Classics 7

Hamlet "The time is out of joint." Soliloquies through the hours. The ghost appears at midnight. Macbeth A creeping, gothic horror clock where the seconds tick down mercilessly as physical blood slowly drips and pools from the digits. The Tempest Shakespeare's unity of time made literal: a live analog dial with the storm's real 2-to-6 window highlighted and split into five acts. The Odyssey 24 books, 24 hours. From the Cyclops to the Sirens to home. Wine-dark seas and ancient Greek glory. The Divine Comedy Dante's journey through Inferno, Purgatorio, and Paradiso. 24 hours descending and ascending. The Canterbury Tales 24 pilgrims, 24 hours. Each tells their tale on the road to Canterbury. Medieval manuscript aesthetic. Don Quixote A sweeping windmill clock featuring a "Lower Visor" toggle that plunges reality into chivalric madness.

The American Century 5

The Great Gatsby A rich, 1920s Art Deco clock where the iconic Green Light pulses relentlessly with every passing second. The Old Man and the Sea Santiago's three-day struggle with the marlin. Hemingway's spare prose, ocean waves, endurance. On the Road Kerouac's fast-paced, jazz-inflected prose tracking a journey across America. Slaughterhouse-Five Billy Pilgrim unstuck in time, jumping between Dresden, Tralfadore, and Ilium. So it goes. Infinite Jest Clock Wallace's subsidized years with footnotes, entertainment cartridges, and E.T.A./Ennet House narratives.

Magic Realism & Metafiction 5

100 Years of Solitude The atmospheric of the collection's two readings of García Márquez: the time blurs and dissolves into memory under Mauricio Babilonia's endless swarm of yellow butterflies. Cien Años de Soledad The structural reading of the same novel: Macondo's circular time drawn as concentric rings, naming which generation of Buendías the hour belongs to. The Library of Babel Set inside an infinite hexagonal grid, exactly one true moment of time is decoded from absolute random alphanumeric chaos every second. If on a winter's night a traveler A meta-narrative clock that directly addresses the Reader, constantly interrupting and completely changing its own visual theme every 15 seconds. House of Leaves A spatially erratic layout where massive disjointed time numbers violently shift across the screen above endless footnotes.

Art 10

Colour, coordinate and concept clocks.

XYZ Clock Hours, minutes and seconds read as x, y and z inside a wireframe cube, trailing the last twelve hours behind the marker. HSV Clock The time as one colour: hours turn the hue through the full spectrum twice a day, minutes set its brightness, seconds its saturation. Spectrum Clock Hour, minute and second each given their own hue, stacked as bands that slide through the spectrum at their own pace. Word Clock A grid of letters that lights only the words it needs, reading the time aloud in English to the nearest five minutes. Polar Clock Hours, minutes and seconds as nested arcs, each sweeping its ring to a close before snapping back to nothing. Bach Clock A barrel organ engraved with the subject of The Art of Fugue. Three voices read the same twelve notes at one per second, per minute and per hour, so the clock's own 1:60:720 ratio becomes a canon by augmentation, and on the hour it plays B flat, A, C, B natural. Eames / Nelson Ball A faithful CSS recreation of George Nelson's iconic mid-century modern atomic ball clock. Prehistoric Deep Time A conceptual simulation of pre-human deep time. Read the time by tracking beautiful calcified text fossils as they are slowly buried beneath layers of falling sediment. Coordinate Clock Assign Y, Mo, D, H, Mi, S to coordinate axes and plot time as a point in Cartesian, Polar, Parabolic, Elliptic, Cylindrical, or Spherical space. DSL Clock A clock you program. A tiny language with its own tokenizer, parser and evaluator decides the text, colour, scale and spin on every frame, and the syntax tree of whatever you type is drawn beside it. Glitch Clock Digits torn into cyan and magenta channels, shivering apart and snapping back on a nervous loop.

Simulation 11

Physics, particle and agent systems that keep time.

Gravity Clock Seconds fall in as balls and pile up, each minute crates them into a box, and the hour clears the floor. Flocking Clock Boids steered by the clock, in four modes: chasing the hour hand, riding a Perlin field the minutes rewrite, harried by a predator that speeds up through the day, or threading between drifting obstacles. Oscilloscope Clock An XY-mode vector trace drawing each digit stroke by stroke, phosphor dimming behind the beam. ODE Clock Particles let loose in a vector field of curl, sources and sinks, the flow redrawn by the passing time. Particle Clock Drifting particles pulled into formation to spell each digit, then scattered loose again when the second turns. Epicycle Clock 3 to 6 nested rotating circles, one per timescale from hours down to hundredths of a second, tracing a glowing Fourier-style rosette. Chaos Pendulum Clock 60 double pendulums forked by a hair, seeded by the hour and minute, blooming into a fresh rainbow of chaos every 60 seconds. State Machine Clock Three nested finite state machines — seconds, minutes, hours — drawn as concentric rings, with each carry visualized as a pulse between rings. Installable as its own offline app.
