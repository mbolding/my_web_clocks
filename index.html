Literary 41

Works of literature reinterpreted as timekeeping devices.

Modernist 8

Bloomsday Clock Bloomsday hour by hour, the real day mapped onto Joyce's 18 episodes at their schema hours, with the time retold in each episode's own style. Finnegans Wake A river dial for Finnegans Wake, Joyce's riverrun turning once a day through Vico's four ages, where the last word flows into the first at dawn. The Waste Land Clock Eliot's five sections as a torn collage of the poem's own hours, from the stroke of nine and the hot water at ten to the violet hour and closing time. Mrs Dalloway Clock Big Ben strikes and its leaden circles spread across 1923 London, handing the day from Clarissa to Septimus to Peter Walsh as each ring reaches them. À la recherche du temps perdu Proust's seven volumes laid across one day with a ribbon bookmark, from going to bed early at midnight to Time Regained, as the same minute on earlier days returns. The Sound and the Fury Four sections, four clocks: Benjy's scattered digits, Quentin's handless watch still ticking, Jason's cotton ticker, and Dilsey reading true time from a one-hand clock. Snow Country Night train window from Kawabata's opening, where the time floats as a faint reflection while snowfields, cedars and distant lamps slide past behind the glass. The Magic Mountain One day is one reading of Mann's novel, seven chapters on a dial where time speeds up, while a thermometer keeps the Berghof's four seven-minute measurements.

Existential & Absurd 7

The Metamorphosis The time read from the alarm clock on Gregor's chest of drawers, seen from his back and then the floor, across a day mapped onto the novella's three parts. The Trial Kafka's Der Process as a court form: the day runs from the arrest at eight to the end at nine, and the time arrives by numbered amendment. The Stranger A day under the Algerian sun: glare and heat shimmer climb toward the beach at two o'clock, then Part Two passes sixteen hours in a cell until the stars. The Sisyphus Clock Camus's boulder, pushed up the mountain each minute in flat cutout collage, then the walk back down, the hour he is aware, and a giant foot on the hour. Crime and Punishment Each hour Raskolnikov walks the 730 steps from his garret to the pawnbroker's bell, while the day runs through the novel and his fever rises and breaks. Catch-22 Clock Yossarian's mission log, 80 eighteen-minute missions a day, and Colonel Cathcart raises the number required to go home just as he gets close. Candide Pangloss insists it is the best of all possible times while Candide's disasters play out hour by hour, Lisbon to Venice, and a garden grows till dusk.

Shakespeare & the Classics 7

Hamlet Hamlet played once a day from a midnight curtain, the Ghost knocks the dial's numerals out of joint and they stay out until Hamlet sets the time right. Macbeth Blood drips from the numerals into a basin that turns from water to red between midnights, as the hour is struck in Lady Macbeth's sleepwalking count. The Tempest Shakespeare's unity of time made literal, the storm fills a real 2-to-6 window in five acts, and the rest of the day tells the twelve years before and voyage after. The Odyssey Homer's 24 books as 24 hours on a sea chart, Odysseus and Telemachus sail in real time while the wanderings replay in flashback from the Phaeacian court. The Divine Comedy Dante's universe in cross-section, the day is the poem's 100 cantos, falling through Hell, up Mount Purgatory, out through the nine heavens. The Canterbury Tales Twenty-four tales, twenty-four hours, as the pilgrims ride sixty miles from the Tabard to Canterbury and the Host reads the hour from the length of a shadow. Don Quixote A La Mancha windmill whose sails tell the minute and the Don's lance the hour, reading Part One by morning and Part Two by night. Lower the visor for giants.

The American Century 5

The Great Gatsby Young Jimmy Gatz's schedule from the back of Hopalong Cassidy runs the day, while across the bay the green light on Daisy's dock burns from dusk to dawn. The Old Man and the Sea Santiago's three days on the Gulf Stream, once a day, in a cross-section of sea: the line under strain, the marlin below, then the sharks strip it to the bone. On the Road The day is Kerouac's scroll, a car runs the novel's four crossings to Mexico City while a typewriter types each minute onto one long roll. Slaughterhouse-Five Billy Pilgrim's life fills the hours out of order, the day drawn at once like a Tralfamadorian mountain range. So it goes. Infinite Jest Clock Wallace's Subsidized Time as a clock, nine sponsored years share the day while 388 endnotes and sub-notes pile up beside the time, E.T.A. vs Ennet House.

Magic Realism & Metafiction 5

100 Years of Solitude The atmospheric reading of the novel: the time is remembered rather than read, fading and coming back, while Macondo's weather follows the hour, from insomnia to the long rain. Cien Años de Soledad The structural reading of the novel: seven Buendía generations spiral inward through the day while Melquíades's parchments are deciphered up to the hour hand. The Library of Babel Every second the time, spelled in the library's 25 symbols, is found on one page of one book on one shelf of one hexagon, with its full address. If on a winter's night a traveler Ten novels begin and break off across the day, one every 2.4 hours, each in its own genre and type, while a chapter addressed to You tells you the time. House of Leaves The house on Ash Tree Lane is a quarter inch bigger inside than out at midnight and the gap doubles every hour, while three narrators footnote the time in their own typefaces.

The Dispossessed Anarres and Urras split by a wall, telling the same instant two ways — communal decads versus mercantile minutes. Ghost in the Shell A tactical cyber-brain interface with high-precision time tracking, data streams, and "Ghost Noise" glitches. Arthur C. Clarke A 2001: A Space Odyssey inspired clock featuring a massive HAL 9000 eye that pulses to the seconds and physically tracks your cursor. LOTR Clock The One Ring resting in the fires of Mount Doom, featuring a glowing Tengwar inscription that crawls with animated fire.

Spell Preparation Clock An illuminated grimoire showing Vancian spells memorizing throughout the day. Art 10

Colour, coordinate and concept clocks.

XYZ Clock Hours, minutes and seconds read as x, y and z inside a wireframe cube, trailing the last twelve hours behind the marker. HSV Clock The time as one colour: hours turn the hue through the full spectrum twice a day, minutes set its brightness, seconds its saturation. Spectrum Clock Hour, minute and second each given their own hue, stacked as bands that slide through the spectrum at their own pace. Word Clock A grid of letters that lights only the words it needs, reading the time aloud in English to the nearest five minutes. Polar Clock Hours, minutes and seconds as nested arcs, each sweeping its ring to a close before snapping back to nothing. Bach Clock A barrel organ engraved with the subject of The Art of Fugue. Three voices read the same twelve notes at one per second, per minute and per hour, so the clock's own 1:60:720 ratio becomes a canon by augmentation, and on the hour it plays B flat, A, C, B natural. Eames / Nelson Ball A faithful CSS recreation of George Nelson's iconic mid-century modern atomic ball clock. Prehistoric Deep Time A conceptual simulation of pre-human deep time. Read the time by tracking beautiful calcified text fossils as they are slowly buried beneath layers of falling sediment. Coordinate Clock Assign Y, Mo, D, H, Mi, S to coordinate axes and plot time as a point in Cartesian, Polar, Parabolic, Elliptic, Cylindrical, or Spherical space. DSL Clock A clock you program. A tiny language with its own tokenizer, parser and evaluator decides the text, colour, scale and spin on every frame, and the syntax tree of whatever you type is drawn beside it. Glitch Clock Digits torn into cyan and magenta channels, shivering apart and snapping back on a nervous loop.

Simulation 11

Physics, particle and agent systems that keep time.

Gravity Clock Seconds fall in as balls and pile up, each minute crates them into a box, and the hour clears the floor. Flocking Clock Boids steered by the clock, in four modes: chasing the hour hand, riding a Perlin field the minutes rewrite, harried by a predator that speeds up through the day, or threading between drifting obstacles. Oscilloscope Clock An XY-mode vector trace drawing each digit stroke by stroke, phosphor dimming behind the beam. ODE Clock Particles let loose in a vector field of curl, sources and sinks, the flow redrawn by the passing time. Particle Clock Drifting particles pulled into formation to spell each digit, then scattered loose again when the second turns. Epicycle Clock 3 to 6 nested rotating circles, one per timescale from hours down to hundredths of a second, tracing a glowing Fourier-style rosette. Chaos Pendulum Clock 60 double pendulums forked by a hair, seeded by the hour and minute, blooming into a fresh rainbow of chaos every 60 seconds. State Machine Clock Three nested finite state machines — seconds, minutes, hours — drawn as concentric rings, with each carry visualized as a pulse between rings. Installable as its own offline app.
