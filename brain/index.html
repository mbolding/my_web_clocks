    Brain Explorer

    An MNI152 viewer with public atlases. Not a clinical tool.

    Getting around

    Click or drag Move the crosshair. Every atlas reports its label in the readout.
    Scroll Step through slices in that view
    Ctrl/⌘ + scroll, pinch Zoom around the pointer
    Shift + drag, right-drag Pan a zoomed view
    ← ↑ → ↓ Nudge the crosshair 1 mm in the active view
    PgUp PgDn Next or previous slice in the active view
    + - 0 Zoom in, out, reset
    [ ] Previous or next atlas
    / Search regions across every atlas
    X Toggle the crosshair

    Your own maps

    Open or drop any NIfTI-1 or NIfTI-2 file (.nii or .nii.gz) that is already in MNI space: a z-map, a mask, a meta-analysis map. It is read in your browser and never uploaded. Files on a different grid are resampled on the fly, trilinear for continuous data and nearest-neighbour for integer data. For 4D files the first volume is shown.
