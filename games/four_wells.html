Four Wells a patience duel

Four Wells

Klondike patience, played as a duel. You start at 30 life. Bring the House to 0 before it does the same to you.

The patience

  • Build columns downward, alternating warm schools (Ember, Dawn) with cool ones (Grove, Tide). Any card or run may fill an empty column.
  • The four Wells build upward by school from the Ace. Tap a card, then tap where it should go. Double-tap sends a card to its Well.
  • Moves are free. Tapping the stock ends your turn: the House plays, then you draw one card.
  • When the stock runs out the waste is turned over, and it costs you 2 life the first time, 4 the next, and so on.

The sorcery

  • Every card in a Well is one mana each turn, of any school. A card put in a Well gives its mana at once.
  • An uncovered card (the last in a column, or the top of the waste) can be cast instead of moved, if its school's Well is open. It leaves the game. The number in the coin is its cost.
  • Ember 2 to 10 burn a target. Dawn mends your life. Grove grows a Sprout creature. Tide freezes the House's strongest creatures, so they can't block now or attack next turn.
  • Knaves (2/2), Queens (3/3) and Kings (4/4) are creatures. Ember ones have Haste, Dawn ones Vigil (attacking doesn't tap them), Grove ones are Sturdy (+2 toughness) and Tide ones are Elusive (can't be blocked).

The fighting

  • Tap one of your creatures and press Attack. It can't attack the turn it arrives, and attacking taps it, so it can't block until your next turn.
  • The House's Guards never attack and always block. Its other creatures attack every turn, and block only when they'd win.
  • When the House attacks, choose blockers: tap your untapped creature, then the attacker. Unblocked damage comes off your life. Damage on creatures heals at the end of each turn.
