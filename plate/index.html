Finder, alignment and timing patterns, plus the format and version strips. Everything else is your content and its error correction. Downloads are never tinted.

Byte mode with UTF-8 text, so accents and emoji work. Version and mask are chosen for you: the version is the smallest grid your content fits, and the mask is whichever of the eight patterns scores best on the readability rules in the spec.
